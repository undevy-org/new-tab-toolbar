# Chrome Web Store Listing — Quiet Tab

## Short description (≤132 characters)

A quiet new tab: your favorite links and local weather. No news feed, no
tracking, nothing else.

## Detailed description

Quiet Tab replaces your new tab page with two things and nothing more:

- **A personal favorites toolbar.** Add, edit, reorder, and delete links
  from a settings panel. Icons come from each site's favicon, a letter
  fallback, or your own image. Favorites sync across your signed-in Chrome
  browsers through Chrome's own built-in sync — there is no project-run
  server.
- **Local weather.** Set a city once and see current temperature, UV index
  (with a WHO-scale level label), today's rain probability, and air
  quality, right on the new tab page.

That's the entire extension. No news feed, no background polling, no
analytics, no tracking of any kind.
